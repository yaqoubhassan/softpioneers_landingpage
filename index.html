Our Work

Featured projects that showcase our expertise and commitment to quality

DriveAssist

AI-Powered Mobile Platform

DriveAssist is an AI-powered two-sided marketplace connecting drivers with automotive experts in Ghana. The app features intelligent vehicle diagnostics using AI APIs (Groq, OpenAI) that analyze symptoms via text or voice input, providing instant diagnoses with confidence scores and cost estimates. An intelligent matching algorithm connects drivers with the best-suited mechanics based on specialization, location, and ratings.

Bukata

Web & Mobile Platform

Bukata began as a DJ booking platform and evolved into a full artisan services marketplace. It connects users with skilled service providers — DJs, photographers, beauticians, and more — enabling easy discovery, booking, and service management.

Availify

Mobile Application

Availify is a location-based mobile app that helps users discover nearby services such as restaurants, salons, hospitals, schools, and hotels. It provides distance and proximity information for quick decision-making.
